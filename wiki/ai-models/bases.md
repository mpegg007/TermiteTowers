# The Bases (The Students) 🧑‍🎓

In our analogy, the **Base** is the underlying architecture or "Student" that is being trained. Just like you can send a smart student to different universities, you can take a base model and fine-tune it for different purposes.

| Base (Student) | Creator | Characteristics | Used In (Variants) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Llama 3.1** (Liberal Arts) | Meta | Excellent NLP, cultural awareness, general reasoning. Speaks naturally. | **Llama 3.1** (Standard), **DeepSeek-R1** (Math Camp), **Hermes 3** (Art School) |
| **Qwen 2.5** (CS Student) | Alibaba | Incredible logic, math, and coding. Often "terse" or direct. | **Qwen 2.5** (Standard), **Qwen2.5-Coder** (Coding), **DeepSeek-R1 7B** (Reasoning) |
| **DeepSeek-V2** (Math Prodigy) | DeepSeek | Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture designed for extreme efficiency and logic. | **DeepSeek-Coder-V2** (Coding Specialist) |
| **Llama 2** (Senior Student) | Meta | Previous gen standard. Solid, but less capable than Llama 3.1. | **CodeLlama** (Coding) |
| **StarCoder2** (Specialist) | BigCode | Built from scratch *only* on code. No "general knowledge" baggage. | **StarCoder2** (Pure Coding) |
| **Phi-3** (Efficiency Expert) | Microsoft | Tiny but mighty. Trained on "textbook quality" data. | **Phi-3.5** (Logic/Math) |
| **GPT-4** (Valedictorian) | OpenAI | Gold standard for general intelligence. Highly capable at everything. | **GPT-4o** (Flagship), **o1** (PhD Student) |
| **Claude 3** (Philosopher) | Anthropic | Thoughtful, nuanced, excellent writer. "Warm" tone, safety-focused. | **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (Coding Favorite), **Claude 3 Opus** (Deep Thinker) |
| **Gemini** (Polymath) | Google | Good at everything, massive context (1M+), native multimodal. | **Gemini 1.5 Pro** (Long Context) |
| **Grok** (Debater) | xAI | Witty, sarcastic ("Fun Mode"), real-time news access (X). Less filtered. | **Grok-2** (Generalist) |
| **Mistral** (Efficient Euro) | Mistral AI | High performance per parameter. Fast, smart MoE models. | **Mistral Large** (Flagship), **Codestral** (Coding) |
