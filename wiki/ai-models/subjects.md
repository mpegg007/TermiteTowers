# The Subjects (The Focus) 📚

In our analogy, the **Subject** is the model's major or specialization. Just like a student can major in Math, English, or Computer Science, models are fine-tuned to excel in specific areas.

| Subject (Focus) | Description | Strengths | Weaknesses | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coding** | Specialized in writing, debugging, and understanding programming languages. | Syntax accuracy, understanding libraries, refactoring, fill-in-the-middle. | Often lacks general world knowledge (e.g., might not know historical facts). | Qwen2.5-Coder, DeepSeek-Coder-V2, CodeLlama, StarCoder2 |
| **STEM** | Focused on hard logic, mathematical reasoning, and scientific principles. | Solving complex equations, step-by-step logic puzzles, algorithmic thinking. | Can be "dry" or terse in conversation. | DeepSeek-R1 (7B), Qwen 2.5 (32B) |
| **Liberal Arts** | A well-rounded education focusing on language, culture, history, and nuance. | Writing essays, summarizing text, understanding cultural context, polite conversation. | May struggle with very complex logic or obscure coding syntax compared to specialists. | Llama 3.1, DeepSeek-R1 (8B - Distill Llama) |
| **Creative / Roleplay** | Focused on storytelling, character embodiment, and creative writing. | Staying in character, writing fiction, generating dialogue, following "uncensored" instructions. | Can "hallucinate" (make things up) more easily when asked for strict facts. | Hermes 3 |
| **Math / Logic** | A sub-set of STEM, often found in smaller models optimized for reasoning. | Logic puzzles, basic math, efficient reasoning. | Limited general knowledge due to small size. | Phi-3.5 |
| **Real-Time / Truth** | Focused on having the most up-to-date information and avoiding filters. | News events, current trends, controversial topics, "spicy" takes. | Can be biased or abrasive depending on the user's perspective. | Grok |
| **Multimodal** | Focused on understanding the world through video, audio, and images. | Analyzing video clips, understanding complex diagrams, listening to audio files. | Sometimes slower than pure text models. | Gemini 1.5 Pro, GPT-4o |
