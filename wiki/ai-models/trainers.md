# The Trainers (The Universities) 🎓

In our analogy, the **Trainer** is the organization or "University" that taught the model. Different organizations have different philosophies, safety standards, and training methodologies.

| Trainer (University) | Philosophy | Known For | Style / Analogy | Models |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meta** (The Dean) | Safety, General Knowledge, Standardization | Llama Series | "Dean's List" students. Well-rounded, polite, standard. | Llama 3.1, CodeLlama |
| **DeepSeek** (Math Dept) | Pure Logic, RL, Efficiency | DeepSeek-R1, Coder-V2 | "Math Prodigies". Excel at complex reasoning and logic puzzles. | DeepSeek-R1, DeepSeek-Coder-V2 |
| **Qwen** (CS Lab) | Performance, Coding, Multilingual | Qwen 2.5 | "CS Majors". Strong at coding/math, punch above weight class. | Qwen 2.5, Qwen2.5-Coder |
| **Nous Research** (Rebel Prof) | Uncensored, Creative, Instruction Following | Hermes Series | "Art School/Rebel". Unlocks models to do exactly what is asked. | Hermes 3 |
| **BigCode** (Vocational) | Transparency, Open Source, Legal Safety | StarCoder | "Vocational School". Trained *only* on permissively licensed code. | StarCoder2 |
| **Microsoft** (Research Inst) | Efficiency, SLMs, Reasoning | Phi Series | "Efficiency Experts". Tiny models with "textbook quality" data. | Phi-3.5 |
| **OpenAI** (Ivy League) | Scale, General Intelligence, Capability | GPT-4, o1 | "Ivy League". Prestigious, powerful, polished, but closed-source. | GPT-4o, o1-preview |
| **Anthropic** (Ethics Comm) | Safety, Constitutional AI, Helpfulness | Claude 3 | "Ethics Board". Smart, nuanced, excellent writers, very careful. | Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Opus |
| **Google** (Mega-Campus) | Multimodal, Massive Context, Integration | Gemini | "Biggest Library". Native multimodal (video/audio) + huge memory. | Gemini 1.5 Pro, Flash |
| **xAI** (Private Incubator) | Truth Seeking, Real-time, Anti-Censorship | Grok | "Private Lab". Witty, rebellious, real-time news access (X). | Grok-2, Grok-3 |
| **Mistral AI** (European Inst) | Efficiency, Open Weights, "Punching Up" | Mistral, Mixtral | "French Powerhouse". Highly efficient, popularized MoE. | Mistral NeMo, Codestral |
